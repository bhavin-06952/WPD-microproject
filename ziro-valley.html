Ziro Valley

Arunachal Pradesh

Ziro Valley is a picturesque valley located in the Lower Subansiri district of Arunachal Pradesh. This beautiful destination is home to the Apatani tribe, known for their unique culture and sustainable agricultural practices.

Why Visit?

The valley offers stunning rice fields that turn golden during harvest season, surrounded by pine forests and mountains. The landscape is breathtaking throughout the year, with each season bringing its own charm.

Main Attractions

- Beautiful rice fields and agricultural terraces

- Apatani tribal villages and their unique culture

- Ziro Music Festival (September) - India's premier outdoor music festival

- Talley Valley Wildlife Sanctuary

- Pleasant weather throughout the year

Best Time to Visit

March to October is ideal, with September being special due to the Ziro Music Festival.

How to Reach

The nearest airport is Lilabari Airport in Assam (about 100 km away). From there, you can hire a taxi to reach Ziro Valley. The journey offers spectacular views of the northeastern landscape.
