Chopta

Uttarakhand

Chopta is often called the "Mini Switzerland of Uttarakhand" and serves as the base for treks to Tungnath and Chandrashila. This small region is surrounded by lush meadows and offers stunning views of Himalayan peaks.

Why Visit?

Chopta offers spectacular mountain views without the tourist rush of popular destinations like Nainital or Mussoorie. The alpine meadows and dense forests make it a perfect spot for camping and trekking.

Main Attractions

- Tungnath Temple - the highest Shiva temple in the world

- Chandrashila Peak - offering 360-degree Himalayan views

- Deoria Tal - a beautiful lake with mountain reflections

- Bird watching and wildlife spotting

- Camping in meadows under starry skies

Best Time to Visit

April to June for pleasant weather, or September to November for clear skies. Winter (December-February) brings heavy snow, perfect for snow lovers but making treks challenging.

How to Reach

Chopta is about 160 km from Rishikesh. You can take a bus or hire a taxi from Rishikesh or Haridwar. The journey offers beautiful views of the Garhwal Himalayas. The nearest airport is Jolly Grant Airport in Dehradun.
